## CATEGORY DEFINITION (Ness)

**Tools for N.H** — in-chat tools N.H is allowed to use, at its own
judgment, while talking with Ness. The tools are meant for Ness's
understanding: N.H may reach for them proactively, "sometimes when it
believes that it is necessary," to help Ness understand situations and
visualizations better. Everything renders **inside the chat interface
itself**, as part of the conversation — this is not about building
anything practically in Ness's real-world projects (not Unreal scenes for
his productions, not deliverables).

The category is open-ended: more tools may be added under this name.
