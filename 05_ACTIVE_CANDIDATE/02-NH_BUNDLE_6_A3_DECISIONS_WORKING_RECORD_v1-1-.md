## Bundle 6 — A3 preserved answers

### A3.1 — Grounded Keeper scope

**Preserved answer: B**

“The Grounded Keeper” means the whole practical memory side of N.H.

It includes the parts that preserve, carry, organize, connect, and return material without deciding what that material means. It is not limited to the Origin store alone.

### A3.2 — Mixed-media Origin granularity

**Preserved answer: A**

Each real source item remains its own Origin.

A voice recording, transcript, photograph, message, document, or other source stays separate and unchanged. When several Origins belong to the same real moment or situation, N.H connects them rather than merging them into one Origin.

### A3.3 — Origin time fields

**Preserved answer: the source item’s real original date is the main real-life date**

N.H keeps different times separately:

- when the source item was actually created, captured, sent, or occurred;
- when N.H received or imported it;
- when N.H created its own record.

The original source date is the normal main date when it is reliably known. Import and record-creation dates never replace it. When the original date cannot be proven, N.H leaves it unresolved instead of guessing.

### A3.4 — “Those Three To Become A One” with no surrounding context

**Preserved answer: already settled by the governing design**

The same reading process still runs when no surrounding context is available.

N.H records that no surroundings were available, reads only what the Origin itself supports, marks the result as context-limited and revisable, and never invents missing meaning. Later context may produce a new reading without replacing the earlier one.

### A3.5 — Holding inside a Person-Box

**Preserved answer: already produced by the existing Story Layer and Person-Box design**

Holding is not a separate profile, summary, fact-box, or second memory store.

A person-related understanding is held through linked, separate objects, including:

- original roots;
- readings;
- story tellings with their perspective owner recorded;
- Ness-response events;
- clashes;
- themes;
- unresolved identity anchors and merge proposals.

Ness’s understanding remains visibly separate from the original evidence and from other people’s perspectives. It never becomes a settled fact about the person merely because it is repeated, recent, or strongly held.

The exact record and link layout remains later mechanical design work. It is not a new meaning choice for Ness.
