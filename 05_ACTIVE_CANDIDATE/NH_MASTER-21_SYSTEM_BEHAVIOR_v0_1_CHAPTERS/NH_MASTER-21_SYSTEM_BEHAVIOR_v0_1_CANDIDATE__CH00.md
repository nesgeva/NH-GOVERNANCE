## 0.1 The machine in one paragraph

DESIGNED — N.H is a local memory system in which eligible input passes through a common intake, raw roots remain preserved, and one meaning engine adds revisable readings beside those roots without turning interpretation into fact. Functions consult the shared mechanism under the applicable permissions while they work; a replaceable language model supplies wording, and visible output passes the privacy gate followed by the access gate.
