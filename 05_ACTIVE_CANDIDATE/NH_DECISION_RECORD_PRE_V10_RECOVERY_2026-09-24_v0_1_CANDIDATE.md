## 4. Decision 3 — Restore both named Wonder walls

**Ness's decision (2026-09-24).** Both walls of the Wonder scratch space are restored by name and by meaning:

**Attribution of the wording.** The archive names the two walls tersely. `98_HISTORICAL_SOURCES_PRE_V10/sources/NH_MASTER-14_FINAL.md`, §7B inline Part 6.5, reads: "Part 6.5 THE WONDER/SIMULATION (kept-and-shown never decided; two walls — file door + eyes door; no scratch→memory path)". **Provenance.**

Scratch-space output cannot enter memory directly. The larger Wonder/simulation mechanism remains incomplete and not built." V10 retains this compressed boundary; the names "file door" and "eyes door" and their two distinct meanings do not appear in V10 (checked by direct comparison at the pinned commit).
- Ledger rows on the Wonder material: **FR-0163** (SUPERSEDED — scratch-space boundary now settled), **FR-0337** (CARRIED — mechanism concept-only), **FR-0412** (CARRIED — Part 6.5 named). **Kept distinct.** These Wonder walls are not the storage protections of S12 — "physical seal + `re_reads` validation" (`NH_DECISION_DEFAULTS-S12.md`, the two-files item). The two pairs of walls protect different things and are not merged by this decision.
