## 4. The approved decision and its eight parts

**`NHD-WR-20260916-3`** — one decision, eight parts, status `ness_decision`:

1. The ordinary chat uses a **normal scrolling conversation**.
2. Its basic behaviour is **ChatGPT-like**.
3. On PC, the chat is **full-window**, with a **smaller presentation when appropriate**.
4. The conversation area is **centred**.
5. Ness's messages appear on the **right**, inside **rounded boxes**.
6. N.H's replies appear on the **left as plain text**, positioned **away from the screen's far-left edge**.
7. **Colours remain undecided.**
8. **Extra features come later.**

One decision ID. The eight parts are not eight decisions and receive no identifiers of their own.

## 5. Consolidated policy meaning

Read together, the eight parts say this and only this:

- The ordinary chat is a **normal scrolling conversation** whose **basic behaviour is ChatGPT-like** — the phrase is Ness's and is carried as he stated it. It is a description of the kind of basic behaviour wanted; it is **not** acceptance of every current or future ChatGPT feature, visual detail, policy, brand element or implementation, and its detailed feature mapping remains open.
- On PC the chat **is full-window** — that is the baseline — **with a smaller presentation when appropriate**. Full-window is neither a fixed resolution nor a mandatory permanent fullscreen mode (the smaller presentation is part of the decision), and "when appropriate" is Ness's condition carried as policy — it is **not** turned into a breakpoint, platform rule, automatic trigger or criterion here.
- The **conversation area itself is the centred element**. This is not reduced to "the whole page is centred."
- **Ness's messages sit on the right in rounded boxes; N.H's replies sit on the left as plain text, kept away from the screen's far-left edge.** "Plain text" is not silently changed into a reply bubble.
- **Colours are undecided.** No palette, theme, or light/dark treatment is chosen.
- **Extra features come later.** This establishes the present design sequence — the ordinary chat's basic appearance and behaviour come first; additional features are later work. "Later" is not "never," and it is not "rejected."

Nothing beyond these sentences is decided by this package.
