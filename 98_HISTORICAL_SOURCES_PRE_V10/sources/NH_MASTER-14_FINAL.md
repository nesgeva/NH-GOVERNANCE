## 0. THE PREMISE — NEVER DECIDE FACTS (NEVER CLOSE THE BOOK)  [DESIGNED — the floor under every rule]

**The line:** The danger N.H exists to stop was never *reasoning* — it was reasoning *hardening into authority*: a living guess freezing into a closed, settled fact. The AI may reason fully — connect, guess, build on its own past reasoning, hold opinions, leave notes, get richer over time, and even wonder/simulate forward at night — and do everything except one thing: **close the book on something into a decided fact.** Every conclusion stays a non-decisive, accreting layer; nothing it concludes ever promotes itself to "real."

**The crystallization:** **N.H is Ness's HELPER, not Ness's DECIDER.** A helper reads, connects, lays out what it sees, even leans — then hands it to the person, who decides. A decider closes the book.

**★ THE SOUL CORRECTION (S11) — three truths held at once, never collapsed:**
1. **Interpretive meaning is never stored as a universal fact — it remains a situated story** (§3G). There is no fact-box for *meaning*. "How real, to whom" is a per-person reading, never a place a meaning goes to become true-for-everyone. *(This is about interpretive meaning, NOT machine-state: provenance and machine facts ARE established — see the §0 S13 sharpening.)*
2. **Ness still decides / affirms.** He is the decider, the membrane. The system does NOT decide what is real — *and neither does it pretend nothing is ever decided.* Ness decides.
3. **★ His deciding is NOT a node in the engine.** It happens out in real life, engine absent. **There is no step after "show."** The engine wonders and shows; the deciding is *off the board.*

**Why a fact is the forbidden thing:** a fact is *the book shut* — true-for-everyone, no longer subjective to Ness. N.H is *his* (הכל יחסי). A fact is the one thing he does not own.

**The crucial distinction:** "never decide facts" does NOT mean "never hold anything firmly." Something can be held with full weight and still never close the book. The enemy was open-vs-closed, not weak-vs-strong.

**★ S13 SHARPENING (from external critique — the wording was too absolute; narrow its JURISDICTION, don't remove the principle):** "never decide facts" must NOT be read as "there are no facts." The clean distinction: **the DUMB machinery MAY establish directly-verifiable machine-state and provenance facts** (this file exists, this hash matches, this record has 8 fields, this role came directly from source, this root-id points here, this write succeeded). **The SMART machinery MAY interpret, connect, and lean — but may NEVER silently promote an interpretation into an established fact.** The authority for any such promotion is Ness, off-board, never the engine. So: *machine-state & provenance truth is decided freely; interpretive meaning is never closed into truth-for-everyone.* Collapsing those two into one prohibition (the old wording) would treat a file hash or a source role as "only a story" — which absurdly WEAKENS the evidence discipline the system exists to protect.

A re-read is a NEW reading beside the old (R6). **FIRST 2c-adjacent build job: a reading-shaped `append_reading` + a reading validator.** **The complex/contract fields, design-settled S13:**

⚠ **BUT THE SHAPE IS NOT FULLY LOCKED — `confidence` is unresolved (§11 item 19).** The record is listed with a single top-level `confidence`, yet §11 item 19 establishes that "confidence" is really FOUR separate quantities (interpretation confidence · source reliability · story firmness · retrieval relevance) and must NOT collapse into one number. So the reading-record SHAPE is **provisionally settled, not design-locked** — `story_layer`/`mode`/unknowns are settled, but the confidence representation (one generic field? `interpretation_confidence` only? a structured object? none at top level?) MUST be resolved BEFORE the validator is written, or the validator would prematurely freeze the very ambiguity S13 just surfaced. **Resolve confidence first → then code the validator/writer.**
- **`story_layer` = a LIST of tellings** (so opposing readings of the same root both stay — clash kept, nothing wins). Each telling: optional `whose · stance · firmness · telling · theme · when`. Empty list = no story-reading yet (honest). Unknown parts OMITTED, never `"unknown"`-filled (absence read at read-time). The top-level `role` (who spoke) ≠ a telling's `whose` (in whose story).
- **`mode` = an OPEN word + a LOCAL confidence** (register: chat/composed/story/question/…). NOT a fixed menu — a menu would re-add a manual gate (R5 / הכל יחסי). One mode per reading; a different reading later is a NEW reading, not an edit. ⚠ **NAMING CLARIFICATION (S13):** `mode`'s confidence is a LOCAL `classification_confidence` (how sure the filter is it named the register right), e.g. `mode: { label: "question", classification_confidence: 0.82 }`. This is NOT the unresolved top-level reading confidence (§11 item 19) — do not let the existence of `mode.classification_confidence` make the next session think the top-level `confidence`/`interpretation_confidence` question is already solved. They are different quantities at different levels; the top-level one is still OPEN.
- **★ `produced_by` = REPRODUCIBILITY-GRADE PRODUCER PROVENANCE (S13 doc-pass; DUMB-machinery structural field, not interpretation).** A structured sub-record stamping WHAT made the reading, enough to approximately explain *why this reading and not another* — not just "which model." Shape (DESIGNED, leave room): `origin` (the information-path origin — at least `"observed"` | `"imported"` | `"simulated"` | `"generated"` | `"reaction"` | `"human_annotation"`, distinguishing whether the input was directly observed, imported from elsewhere, produced in the simulation/wonder space, generated by the engine, derived from Ness's reaction to an earlier N.H output, or hand-annotated) · `model` (name) · `model_digest` (hash/exact version, not just name) · `engine_version` · `prompt_version` (template) · `config` (decoding/settings relevant to reproduce) · the retrieval inputs/context-ids+order the reading was built from (so a reading is approximately recreatable). **For `origin:"human_annotation"` (a GOLD annotation): the model fields give way to ANNOTATION provenance** — `annotator` · `annotated_at` · `context_version` · `change_reason` (why it changed between gold versions). **Validator requirement:** `produced_by` is REQUIRED on every engine-generated reading (without it you can't tell a stale reading from a current one, can't re-evaluate an old batch when the mouth is swapped, can't attribute a bad reading to a model version). Gold cases satisfy it via the human-annotation form — they are NOT exempt from provenance, only from MODEL provenance. This is the structural anchor that makes item 19's "no silent confidence inheritance / no false corroboration" enforceable.
- **★ `derived_from` = LINEAGE (S13 doc-pass; first-schema PIN — un-retrofittable, must exist from the first reading).** A list of PARENT READING ids the reading was derived from; **empty `[]` when the reading was produced fresh directly from source roots** (those roots are in `reads`). `produced_by.origin` says what KIND of information path; `derived_from` says WHICH prior readings sit upstream. Together they let the path be reconstructed later. **THE GOVERNING RULE (records here + item 19): derived material must never count as INDEPENDENT evidence for the interpretation that produced it; repeated readings descending from one lineage remain ONE evidence path, not multiple confirmations.** This blocks the invisible confirmation loop — interpret a root → discuss it → the discussion enters as new data → the discussion gets read as independent support for the original interpretation — where one guess wears several costumes and certainty climbs while evidence doesn't (a §0 soul violation that no "fact" field would catch). **The FIELDS are pinned now; the full lineage analysis + independence-weighting (walking `derived_from` to discount downstream agreement) is DEFERRED** (beside item 19 / the view layer) — the slot must exist, the logic can come later.
- **★ `idempotency_key` = OPERATION identity, separate from `id` the RECORD identity (S13 doc-pass; writer-contract PIN).** `id` is the unique IMMUTABLE identity of the stored reading. `idempotency_key` (a.k.a. generation-run id) is the stable identity of the WRITE OPERATION, reused when a crashed/repeated command is retried; **the writer rejects an already-committed key.** A content hash over `reads`+`meaning`+`produced_by`+`schema_version` may ALSO be stored for exact-duplicate/integrity detection, but it must NOT carry the retry guarantee — a nondeterministic mouth can rephrase `meaning` on replay (hash misses the dup), and two legitimately independent readings can collide (hash invents a dup). Operation-identity does the retry job; record-identity is permanent; content-hash is an optional integrity extra.
