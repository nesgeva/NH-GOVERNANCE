## NEVER RE-ASK — S17 CONCEPTUAL DECISIONS

### Catalog Front Door and pre-ingest
- Raw capture and root ingestion are two distinct gates.
- Every front door supplies the minimum intake envelope and never guesses missing values.
- One unified pre-ingest store uses seven lifecycle states: `held`, `ready`, `promoting`, `promoted`, `rejected`, `excluded`, `error`.
- A blocked item does not block unrelated items; promotion is idempotent and atomic.
- `role` comes from source or explicit resolution, never silent inference.
- `source_title` is a grouping key, never a generated semantic summary.
- Source facts, deterministic derivations, machine proposals, and semantic interpretation are four separate enrichment categories.
- Privacy/exclusion rules override catalog preservation for secrets, exclusions, redaction, deletion, and mixed-content separation.

### Context Retrieval
- Positional and semantic retrieval are separate labeled channels.
- Similarity is not conversational position and never silently overrides it.
- Parameters belong to reading modes, not one universal constant.
- Retrieval is bounded and auditable.
- Genuine empty retrieval and retrieval-system failure are different states.
- Engine B’s `n=3` is an experiment configuration, not the universal future positional limit.

### Meaning Engine Interior
- One pass produces one reading record for one target root.
- Flow: root/context/mode → mouth proposal → Reading Proposal Acceptance Check → reading record.
- **Model confidence is metadata, not authority.**
- The mouth does not approve its own output.
- Story-layer root evidence and prior-reading context remain separate.
- Circular derived support is forbidden.
- Conflicting readings remain visible.

### Reread Lifecycle
- Only three trigger classes exist: manual, materially relevant condition-based, and bounded scheduled retry for temporary technical failure.
- Time passing alone does not trigger reinterpretation.
- A reread creates a new reading with an explicit recorded reason and never overwrites history.

### View Layer and Computed View
- View Layer has current and history views.
- Per-root current lists may show the newest usable reading first, but that grants no authority.
- Any claim to show the current best-supported picture uses Computed View’s seven-factor order; recency is only a tie-breaker.
- Computed View snapshots are triggered, immutable, auditable, and may say “no clear current view.”
- Held pre-ingest content is metadata-only unless a future authorized inspection mode is designed.

### Contradiction and Clash Handling
- Six clash types remain distinct.
- Triggered and periodic/on-demand detection produce the same conceptual clash type.
- Detection history and refinement are append-only linked events, not mutation.
- Ness’s response is a separate event; downstream action is another separate record.
- N.H surfaces clash and never silently selects a winner.

### Story Layer
- A telling and an ongoing story are different.
- `root_speaker`, `subject`, and `perspective_owner` are distinct roles.
- Firmness and model confidence are separate.
- Themes are hybrid: engine proposes, Ness confirms; repetition does not confirm.
- Story Layer never merges conflicting tellings into one authoritative narrative.
- The future telling identity/storage seam remains open; do not assume standalone telling IDs.

### Person-Boxes
- Person-Boxes are identity anchors and gathers, never synthesized personality profiles.
- Creation and identity merge are proposal-based.
- Uncertain references may remain separate indefinitely.
- The default view has seven evidentially distinct sections.
- Pre-ingest links are metadata-only.
- Historical objects are linked, never rewritten by a merge.

### Living State Web
- Its status is partial conceptual design, not complete and not “not designed.”
- Node/edge families, grounding, currency, action surfacing, result return, and major system relationships are designed.
- Currentness and existence are separate.
- Time alone may make a state stale/unknown but may not prove it ended.
- Causal and counterfactual material remains explicitly hypothetical.
- The web maps possible movement; it never chooses the path.

### Action Surfacing and Result Return
- Suggestions are possibilities, never instructions.
- On-request and explicitly authorized proactive surfacing are the two modes.
- Ness-response states are possibility-disposition states: accepted/acted, rejected, modified, postponed, ignored, alternatives requested.
- Suggestion, action, result root, and result connection remain separate objects.
- Two result types and six result states are settled.
- Success, partial success, and failure are confirmed only through Ness’s judgment; otherwise N.H may record a revisable appearance-consistent interpretation without proving causation.
- Timing and similarity alone never prove an action caused an outcome.

### Permission and Authority
- Suggesting, preparing, and executing are separate.
- Four risk levels and two authority layers are settled.
- Standing permission cannot silently authorize broad external execution.
- Recurring authorization must be narrow, explicit, expiring/revocable, and separately recorded.
- Changed conditions, ambiguity, expired permission, or unexpected output require stop and reconfirmation.
- Authority violation defaults to stop-and-surface.
- Corrective action is a new action requiring its own authority.
- The emergency-stop exception prevents further effects only; it does not authorize completed-world reversal.

### Privacy, Deletion, and Sensitive Data
- The area is partially conceptually designed; do not call it wholly undesigned or fully complete.
- Exclusion, hiding, restriction, redaction, and deletion are distinct.
- Four sensitivity levels are settled.
- Ness’s authenticated private access is open by default unless an explicit restriction applies.
- External exposure and secondary uses are restricted by default.
- Privacy eligibility is checked before retrieval/ranking; output privacy is checked again before display or external use.
- Live credentials and equivalent secrets never enter normal stores.
- Third-party data keeps source/perspective distinctions and may not become a fixed profile.
- Deletion is blocking and verification-based; only `verified_complete` may be called complete deletion.
- A tombstone may preserve the deletion event but never reconstructive content.
- Model-provider refusal is a mouth limitation, not an N.H privacy rule or truth judgment.

### Interface, World, and Interaction
- The world is named **Ness’s World**.
- Conversation mode and World mode are named; automatic presentation changes must not secretly start actions or simulations.
- Opening Ness’s World requires identity confirmation and then Ness’s confirmation.
- The default doorway is a plain white wooden door.
- Chat is N.H’s movable presence inside the world, not a fixed sidebar.
- N.H has no single fixed form; visual form and voice may vary independently.
- Replay and simulation are separate. Simulation never begins secretly; simulated voice is introduced with “Simulating voice.”
- World manipulation is presentation/navigation/simulation-only by default and does not rewrite underlying records or external systems.
- The strongest current world concept remains provisional, not a fully settled specification.
