# N.H — Interface, Chat, World, Voice, VR, and Simulation Design Log

**Status:** Paused by Ness  
**Purpose:** Preserve the interface/world-mode design discussion in one file.  
**Important:** Conceptual design only. Nothing is built. Unanswered items remain open.

---

## Core Direction

N.H should feel like entering **Ness’s own mind or world**, not like opening a normal app with chat inside it.

The world is called:

# Ness’s World

It can contain and grow to contain memories, people, places, projects, states, stories, unfinished things, possible futures, simulations, and many more categories still to be designed.

---

## Modes and Switching

Named so far:

- **Conversation mode** — one clear place where N.H speaks with Ness.
- **World mode** — Ness moves through his inner world.
- **Automatic switching** — N.H may change presentation when the situation clearly benefits.
- **Manual switching** — Ness can command changes through chat or voice.
- More modes are expected and remain undesigned.

Automatic switching may change presentation, but must not secretly begin a new action or simulation.

---

## Opening Ness’s World

Ness may say:

> “Open Ness’s World.”

A normal command alone is not enough.

Opening requires identity confirmation through:

- a private code; or
- thumb/fingerprint verification.

After verification, N.H says:

> “Identity confirmed. Open Ness’s World?”

Ness then confirms.

Still open:

- exact visual transition;
- same-screen versus full-screen opening;
- desktop/mobile/VR differences.

---

## Deep Structure

Ness’s World must allow extremely deep nesting, for example:

Ness’s World  
→ People  
→ Family  
→ Mother  
→ Specific period  
→ Specific event  
→ Specific conversation  
→ Specific sentence  
→ Specific meaning, evidence, state, or unanswered question

The current category list is far too small. The category system remains open and must eventually support many more branches, sub-branches, and ways of reaching the same object.

---

## Initial State and Growth

Ness’s World begins as a blank space.

As Ness types, speaks, imports, creates, remembers, and interacts:

- memories appear;
- people appear;
- places appear;
- projects appear;
- paths and categories form;
- the world keeps growing;
- the structure can change over time;
- Ness can reorganize or transform it;
- N.H may also reorganize parts automatically under rules still to be designed.

---

## World Properties — Settled So Far

- The world is **infinite**.
- Ness may move by:
  - walking;
  - flying;
  - instantly pulling himself toward things.
- New things may appear automatically, but Ness may also place them manually.
- Visual form changes depending on content.
- Whether the world has a stable home or center depends on Ness’s mood.
- The world itself reacts to Ness’s mood/state.
- Weather, lighting, and sound may change with Ness’s state.
- Categories may appear as places, objects, beings, portals, or whatever fits.
- Areas may reorganize automatically over time.
- Important things may glow or call to Ness.
- Multiple versions of the same thing may exist in different places.
- When Ness goes deep into one branch, the visibility of the rest of the world may change depending on the situation.
- Ness may grab, move, merge, split, and reshape parts of the world by hand.
- Time may move normally, pause, rewind, or speed up.
- Moving between related spaces should feel smooth.
- Moving between unrelated spaces may use a clear doorway.
- Ness may pause automatic world changes.
- Whether manually placed objects stay fixed depends on rules/configuration.

---

## The White Door

The default doorway is always a **plain white wooden door**.

It does not automatically change with the theme.

The door is not mainly for objects entering the world. Ness enters through it.

The current concept:

- Ness moves through doors into spaces he is creating or exploring.
- Previous spaces remain behind him.
- The last two generated spaces should remain especially easy to return to.
- Spaces may be built using memories.
- Older spaces may remain farther back, but exact navigation is not settled.

Still open:

- whether every generated space starts blank;
- whether some spaces begin partly formed from memory/context;
- whether Ness chooses blank / memory-built / mixed;
- whether N.H may choose automatically;
- whether Ness can override instantly by voice;
- exact backtracking order;
- whether each recent space has its own direct door.

## Chat as N.H’s Presence

Chat should remain available inside Ness’s World.

It should not have to be a fixed sidebar.

It may be:

- movable;
- draggable;
- resizable;
- brought closer;
- pushed away;
- partly hidden;
- summoned by voice;
- dismissed by voice.

The chat is not merely a text box. It is **N.H’s presence** inside Ness’s World.

N.H may appear as:

- a person;
- a floating panel;
- an object;
- a voice with no visible form;
- another form that fits the situation.

N.H has no single fixed form.

N.H may change form:

- automatically;
- when Ness commands it;
- in response to mood;
- in response to purpose;
- in response to environment;
- in response to Ness’s state;
- according to the type of help needed.

N.H’s visual form and voice may change independently.

---

## Reading the Room

N.H should read the room.

- Small visual changes may happen naturally.
- Big changes should ask Ness first.
- The request should not ruin the moment.

Instead of a cold popup, N.H may say:

> “I could open this into a space around us.”

or:

> “This might be easier if I take form here.”

N.H may ask through:

- voice;
- a small gesture;
- light;
- a quiet symbol;
- hand tracking;
- subtle movement in the space.

If N.H reads the room incorrectly, Ness may correct it immediately by voice.

Depending on the moment, N.H may:

- adjust immediately and silently;
- briefly acknowledge;
- ask one short clarification.

---

## Voice

N.H can speak and listen by voice.

Ness may summon, silence, dismiss, or turn N.H off through voice.

N.H’s voice may change in:

- pace;
- warmth;
- distance;
- intensity;
- presence;
- emotional tone;
- role/persona.

Mostly, it should still feel like the same N.H underneath.

It may intentionally sound different when Ness asks it to:

- replay or approximate a remembered person’s voice;
- represent a specific person in a memory;
- reconstruct how something may have sounded;
- use a chosen different persona.

Two distinct modes:

- **Replay mode** — actual recorded or documented words.
- **Simulation mode** — new generated words in a requested voice, only when Ness asks.

Before simulated voice begins, N.H says:

> “Simulating voice.”

That phrase starts in N.H’s normal voice and shifts smoothly and gradually into the simulated voice.

The visual form may shift at the same time, but does not have to.

---

## N.H Reacting to Ness

N.H’s appearance may react to Ness’s state even while silent.

The strength of the reaction may vary automatically depending on the moment.

Ness does not want a fixed subtle/balanced/obvious setting.

N.H should learn from Ness’s corrections gradually.

One correction must not instantly become a permanent rule.

N.H should not automatically announce every new pattern it notices.

Ness must be able to inspect what N.H has learned later.

That inspection place should show:

- current learned patterns;
- earlier patterns;
- how each changed;
- what evidence caused the change;
- current certainty.

Ness does not want to manually rewrite the entire learned model.

He wants to inspect it and provide small corrections through conversation.

Ness’s exact correction words must be preserved as direct evidence, separate from N.H’s interpretation.

Still open:

- whether the full context of each correction is also preserved automatically;
- how learned interaction patterns are displayed;
- how Ness challenges or limits one without directly editing the whole model.

Desired future interaction methods:

Possible actions include:

- whether camera mode mirrors VR mode.

## Simulation

Simulation may involve:

- memories;
- reconstructed voices;
- people;
- possible futures;
- environments;
- alternative choices;
- remembered situations;
- generated spaces.

A simulated voice is introduced with:

> “Simulating voice.”

Actual replay and simulation remain separate.

Simulation must not begin secretly.

Entering simulation is a major change and requires Ness’s approval, but the request should preserve the moment.

Still substantially undesigned:

- how simulation starts;
- whether it opens through a white door;
- whether it changes the whole world or one area;
- first-person vs observer vs both;
- time controls;
- branching and reset;
- simulated people;
- N.H’s form inside simulation;
- memory replay vs simulation visual difference;
- evidence/reconstruction/invention labeling;
- instant exit;
- emotional-overload handling;
- saving and revisiting simulations;
- whether simulation outputs become plans or remain possibilities;
- hand tracking and voice inside simulation.

Ness moves through it physically or instantly, manipulates it directly, travels between distinct spaces through plain white wooden doors, and can always summon N.H as a changing presence through voice, form, panel, person, object, or environment.
