## 4. The three decisions, individually identified

**NHD-WR-20260721-1.** When a new individual room is created, it may begin **blank**, **built from memories**, or **mixed**. **Ness or N.H** may choose the starting form.

**NHD-WR-20260721-2.** **Ness's direct choice overrides N.H's automatic choice.**

**NHD-WR-20260721-3.** A **clear voice command** from Ness changing how the room begins **takes effect immediately, without another confirmation**.

Status of each: `ness_decision` (index Part G.1). Not re-asked. Not changed. Not renumbered.

## 5. Consolidated policy meaning

Read together, the three decisions say this and only this:

- A new individual room has **three permitted starting forms** — blank, built from memories, mixed. They are **options, not a chosen default**. No form is preferred by this package.
- **Two choosers are permitted**: Ness directly, or N.H automatically. "May" is kept as "may": N.H is permitted to choose automatically, but **when, under what conditions, and by what means** N.H would do so is not decided here.
- When the two choosers conflict, **Ness's direct choice always wins** over N.H's automatic choice.
- When Ness gives a **clear voice command** to change the new room's starting form, the change **takes effect immediately** and **no further confirmation is asked**. "Immediately" and "clear" are Ness's words carried as policy; neither is turned into a technical threshold or test here.

Nothing beyond these four sentences is decided by this package.

## 6. Boundaries and non-effects

- **New individual room only.** This policy concerns how a new individual room begins. It does **not** concern the initial creation or opening of Ness's World as a whole, which stays governed by Master §19A: the world itself begins blank and grows from Ness's life (line 3038), and opening it requires identity confirmation → "Identity confirmed. Open Ness's World?" → a separate Ness confirmation (line 3036). Those world-level rules are untouched.
- **Not the Create-New-World door.** The A19 checkpoint v1.2 §7 records a separate creation path (a real spatial door leading into a blank white room). That is a different decision about a different path; this package neither merges with it nor changes it.
- **"Without another confirmation" is narrow.** It applies only to changing a new room's starting form through a clear voice command. It does **not** remove or weaken any existing identity, access, world-entry, simulation, replay, Wonder, or external-action permission boundary (Master §7P; §19A including the opening rule at line 3036 and simulation basics at line 3056; §25; §26). Voice recognition, speaker verification and identity rules are unchanged.
- **Memory-built and mixed rooms do not touch memory.** Building a room from memories, or mixing, is presentation of existing memory material. It does **not** change, merge, confirm, promote or rewrite roots, readings, tellings, Person-Boxes, Living State records, permissions, deletion state or privacy state (Master §0; §7Q — deletion is non-destructive visibility removal with a history record, lines 2428 and 2455, so nothing a room presents may alter deletion or privacy state; §19A world-manipulation boundary at line 3042; UE5 §2.3 as a consistent restatement). Which memories are involved, and how, is not decided here.
- **No default, no obligation.** The package adds no exception, threshold, prerequisite or ordering to the three decisions.
- **No implementation permission.** Nothing here authorizes code, stores, migrations, disk changes or a build.

## 7. Open dependencies

Preserved as open, unanswered, and unbiased:

1. when and under what circumstances N.H may choose a starting form automatically;
2. how the three options are represented or selected;
3. memory selection, provenance and privacy handling for memory-built and mixed rooms;
4. the composition and meaning of "mixed";
5. the exact point in room creation at which a choice becomes active;
6. voice recognition, speaker verification, and the technical test for a "clear" command;
7. storage, operation identity, state transition, retry, recovery and duplicate prevention;
8. UI, accessibility and presentation behaviour (desktop, mobile, VR);
9. behaviour when a command is unclear, incomplete, conflicting, or technically fails;
10. whether any broader behaviour applies to reopening or changing an already-started room;
11. later Master and Map currentness corrections (see §8);
12. all other A19 and Bundle 7 work.

Later mechanics belong to their existing future design owners under the accepted plan and the Map (A19 / Bundle 7 for experience and policy; the relevant mechanical packages and Bundle 8 for wiring). No new owner and no new identifier is assigned by this package.
