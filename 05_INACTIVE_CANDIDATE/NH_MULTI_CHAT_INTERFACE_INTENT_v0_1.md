# NH_MULTI_CHAT_INTERFACE_INTENT_v0_1.md — Ness's intent: several chats side by side, dragging between them, focus mode, and a voice box

**What this is.** Ness's new interface idea, recorded in his words on 2026-10-05. **It is an intent, not a design.** It decides nothing, adds nothing to Master-21, and is designed later through the normal route. Prepared by Claude at Ness's request. Status: intent, for `05_INACTIVE_CANDIDATE/`.

## 1. Ness's words

- "in the interface, the couple of things i'm able to talk about with n.h at the same time to be a couple of saperate chats that i could drag boxes and stuff from one to another also, and also be able to connect them by dragging stuff and telling \"take from this chat and put into this\" and it will be a command that it will agree to do without needing to click (if it doesn't seem like it would do anything bad or ruin anything) also, the other chats could connect and show icons in the top of the chat"
- "I could also go fullscreen in 1 chat and off full screen (which would put me in a specific chat and focus on the specific chat"
- The voice box: "it says it in a voice box/smaller chat in the side (that all of the voice is being read from and i will be able to see it as well in the smaller chat)"

In short:
- several separate chats at once, one per thing Ness is talking about;
- dragging boxes and things between chats;
- connecting chats by dragging, or by saying "take from this chat and put into this", which runs without a click if it looks harmless;
- connected chats show icons at the top;
- full screen for one chat (focus) and back;
- a small side voice box showing everything N.H says aloud.

## 2. What Master-21 already has (C-19 — Interface / Ness's World)

- **C-19.1 — Conversation and World modes:** "Keeps one clear conversational place distinct from moving through Ness's World" (V10 §19A).
- **C-19.1.4 — Manual mode switching,** by a deliberate chat or voice command.
- **Not yet checked:** whether "one clear conversational place" allows several parallel chats. This is the first thing to check when this intent is designed.

## 3. Open questions (Ness's; they join his question queue)

1. **Separate chats:** do they share N.H's memory, or keep things apart until Ness connects them?
2. **What "connect" does:** copy, move or link? Does a connection stay?
3. **"Without a click if harmless":** who judges harmless, and how does that fit his answer that anything which changes his things needs his OK? (See `NH_VOICE_COMMANDS_INTENT_v0_2.md` §5.)
4. **What the icons show:** for example, which chats are connected, or new activity in them.
