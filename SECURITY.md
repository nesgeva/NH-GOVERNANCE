# Privacy, access, and action

Protection rules constrain every component and output channel.

## Privacy

- Ness's authorized private access is open by default. Sensitive, medical, emotional, or uncomfortable subject matter alone does not justify blocking or softening truthful understanding.
- External exposure and secondary use are restricted by default. Storage permission is not interpretation, disclosure, sharing, or action permission.
- Keep sensitivity levels separate from protected-handling tiers. Level-1 protected raw content and its physical paths stay outside ordinary components and logs. Use opaque references.
- Capture exclusion sends excluded raw material to separate sealed protected storage. Ordinary Catalog, TSC, memory, and archives retain only permitted remainder and non-reconstructive exclusion metadata. Protected preservation is not destruction.
- Non-negotiable live credentials and secrets require protected handling. Do not make all sensitive personal material automatically excluded.
- Keep exclusion, sealed isolation, hiding, restriction, redaction, and deletion from ordinary visibility distinct. Internal influence removal is separate. Hiding or deleting from view does not destroy originals or stop internal influence.
- Explicit influence removal names scope, affected components, and start time. Stop that use from the enforcement commit. Never infer broader scope from a topic pause.
- Track supplied sources and dependencies through derived records. Treat supplied material as potentially influential, not proof of its exact effect on reasoning. Restrictions block scoped retrieval and dependent summaries, connections, indexes, caches, and unfinished work. Discard affected drafts and generation context. Asking a model to ignore restricted material still in its input is insufficient.
- Rebuild derivatives from permitted independent evidence through their normal owner and acceptance rules. Preserve originals and history under their access rules. Removing a sentence may leave other influence. Independent instructions remain usable within scope. Removal does not establish an opposite belief or undo delivered words or completed actions. Disclose unverified dependency coverage.
- Understand natural instructions. A clear reopening needs no repeated confirmation. Do not silently restore a paused topic.
- Visible deletion includes direct and indirect traces and safely rebuilt derivatives. Verify every relevant surface before claiming completion. Preserve honest partial, failed, or unknown outcomes and a content-free record of what occurred.
- Master-21's visibility-removal outcomes are `protected_complete`, `protected_with_declared_limits`, `incomplete`, `blocked`, and `failed`. Do not reuse older destructive-deletion wording. Report remaining traces, unverified locations, limits, and possible further action where incomplete.
- During scoped hiding, verified surfaces retain their result; unverified surfaces withhold. History and expanded views cannot bypass privacy.
- Protected transfer creates an opaque identity, writes and verifies protected raw content, replaces ordinary exposure with the reference, verifies ordinary locations, then completes. Block visible output throughout. Keep topic-pause, visibility, influence, restoration and supersession instructions distinct.
- Hiding requires acknowledgments for chat, inspection, search, Story Layer, Person-Boxes, Living State Web, Computed View, simulation, notifications, exports, tool output, shared screens and external delivery. Missing acknowledgment never permits output. Independently re-query registered derivative, sealed-record, backup and retained-model-context locations before claiming completion.
- Backups use their supported visibility marking, restore exclusion or scheduled supersession. Apply current visibility/influence instructions before restored use. Until each affected point verifies a restoration, enforce the stricter old/new rule.
- Third-party speech stays attributed. Separate actual words/actions, Ness's interpretation, and N.H's interpretation. Do not infer private states as facts, build fixed psychological profiles, or treat closeness as disclosure permission.
- Ordinary bounded interpretations of another person need no extra simulation approval. Speaking as that person, using their voice, extended enacted exchanges, detailed branching reactions or an interactive hypothetical mind require clear prior approval. No extra gate follows solely from collection size or a person being a minor or vulnerable. Ordinary privacy, evidence, legality, harm and sharing rules remain; Ness may choose stricter person/group rules.
- Sensitivity categories are general personal, sensitive personal, third-party/relationship, and credentials/secrets/high-harm. Their numbering is separate from protected-handling tiers. The raw-versus-derived choice is per data type, not a universal biometric rule. Exact quotations, including reported third-party words, still require current access and privacy permission.
- Only explicitly authorized applications may access personal data. The recorded app-only direction excludes an ordinary browser by default. Keep sensitive content out of intermediate buffers, caches and temporary files outside the authorized path. Approved phone copies retain their separate scope.

## Modes and speaker access

Effective permission is the intersection of deliberate mode, privacy, current SACL ceiling, and applicable BAI authority. The strictest applicable rule wins.

- Dry Mode cannot reach Personal stores through code. UI appearance alone is insufficient separation.
- Personal Mode requires deliberate opening, a PIN or purpose-bound biometric proof, current owner checks, committed activation records, and release of the delivery fence before private retrieval.
- Recognition alone does not open Personal Mode. Opening does not grant top-security. Step-up remains a separate authority.
- Recognized-Ness is not a mandatory factor for every ordinary opening. Missing voice or an unrelated BAI outage does not invalidate PIN-only opening. A valid gate with unsafe current speaker access opens suspended, without private retrieval. A valid suspended session may resume future work after fresh checks; discarded output never resumes. Loss of top-security alone may leave ordinary Personal Mode active.
- Activate runtime state under its reserved generation with the delivery fence closed. Flush the truthful opened event, commit operation and generation, then release delivery. Restriction applies even when logging fails. A SACL snapshot represents one committed multistream state; owner or observability changes invalidate it.
- Mode states are `dry`, `personal_opening`, `personal_active`, `personal_suspended`, `personal_closing`, and `personal_closed`.
- Bind access to runtime epoch and generation. At most one live/nonterminal session exists per boundary. Restart creates a new epoch and starts Dry. Never restore sessions, raw factors, prior recognition, tokens, leases, or dead-epoch output from history.
- On access reduction, stop new forbidden retrieval and discard affected output before delivery. Preserve independently authorized work and durable records. Resume surviving work only under current bindings.
- Closing immediately blocks private retrieval and delivery, releases transient buffers, and requests any separate authority ending through its actual owner. Indicators follow committed state and grant no authority.

SIA assesses each speaker stream independently. SACL decides access. BAI supplies purpose-bound biometric proof. None may diagnose wellbeing or borrow another's authority.

Access levels are `guest`, `known_person`, `recognized_ness`, and `top_security`.

Initial PBR permission categories are family information, health information, N.H project information, creative projects, current plans, shared memories and practical information. These names grant no access by themselves. A person cannot inspect their full Person-Box, hidden readings/observations, recognition workings or stored-volume details. Loss of a required recognized-Ness presence drops the dependent person's stream to guest. Failed/stale SACL or PBR lookup also fails to guest; ambiguous profile candidates stay unresolved.

- Guests get natural responses from permitted material without clues that hidden material exists.
- Known people receive only their current Person-Box permission categories and required Ness-presence conditions. One person's permission never transfers to another. Other people cannot change N.H memory, behavior, permissions, security, or authority.
- Keep each parent as a separate identity. Parent translation follows Ness's request and produces an attributed, revisable reading. Private wording help does not authorize speaking to the parent or intervening in the conversation.
- Shared or observable output uses the minimum access of active streams. Use an individual's ceiling only on an owner-confirmed private, unobservable path. Personal Mode alone cannot make a shared channel private.
- SACL continuously recalculates access on speaker changes, uncertainty, stale assessments, and security events. Check categories and presence again at output time.
- Voice is a soft factor, not a hard lockout or sole identity proof. Identity uncertainty cannot increase access.
- Acoustic spoofing and behavioral `imitation_risk` are separate. Low acoustic suspicion reduces the stream to guest without an alert. Medium/high also relocks top-security and queues a private alert. Ordinary identity uncertainty produces no private spoofing alert. Behavioral divergence does not remove ordinary recognized-Ness access under Option A; it blocks top-security where specified.
- Wellbeing tiers, appointment anchors, emotional readings, and behavioral divergence are not identity or access evidence.

## Biometrics and owner recovery

- The OS owns raw biometrics. BAI receives results, never fingerprint images/templates. Bind purpose and requester before prompting. Keep one pending request with a random challenge and trusted local expiry.
- On matched success, issue either an exact one-time token or a revocable top-security lease. Tokens are consumed once before the protected effect. Leases support repeated checks until revoked or expired.
- Reject expired, mismatched, malformed, delayed, duplicated, or already resolved results. Do not retry a failed biometric prompt automatically. Clear volatile authority on restart.
- Top-security needs both current SACL Gate 1 and a live compatible BAI lease. Voice alone, biometric success alone, or old recorded proof is insufficient.
- Separate keys by token purpose. Keep hardware-backed manifest-signing and rollback-sealing keys independent and nonexportable.
- BOP records only the physical prompt/result event. Tokens, purposes, challenges, consumption, and security consequences belong to security audit.

Initial pairing progresses through QR available, provisional pairing with the QR permanently inert, recovery setup, and finalized owner setup. Never regenerate the used QR or treat scanning as final trust.

Normal recovery codes appear only on the paired phone. Verify the Bitwarden save and local acceptance before activating a new code; then invalidate the old code and seal the local copy. Do not expose code values on the desktop or clipboard, or retain save-check inputs/results.

Encrypt the code to the phone's hardware-backed key and require biometric decryption. Display for 10 minutes 30 seconds or until confirmation. Verify four unique randomly chosen character positions in requested order, case-sensitively, locally. Allow three total attempts with fresh positions after failure. After three failures, permanently inert the new code and preserve the old one. A non-effecting local acceptance test precedes activation. Bitwarden is the intended long-term home.

Replacement requires the normal recovery code and local thumbprint. Fully verify the new phone before revoking the old one, then rotate recovery material. Emergency recovery is local, provisional until its required preparation completes, and atomic across new trust, old revocations, old-material invalidation, and new-material activation. It grants no code or configuration editing authority.

Emergency recovery requires physical desktop access, desktop thumbprint, a separate emergency code outside Bitwarden and a securely stored printed recovery sheet. Never perform it remotely. Existing trust and recovery material remain valid provisionally. Save and verify the new normal code, emergency code and sheet before the atomic replacement. Failure before commitment inerts new provisional material and preserves the prior valid set.

Before relying on encrypted backups, define and test desktop failure, phone loss, and combined-loss recovery. Specify owner proof, recovery-material custody, replacement trust, and lost-device revocation. Do not assume recovery codes decrypt backups. Declare unrecoverable cases before use. Backup copies grant no device trust.

Restore into a fresh Dry epoch under the mode rules. Recover current restrictions and device revocations through a protected mechanism beyond the older snapshot. If freshness cannot be established, withhold affected material. Verify record integrity, references, and restrictions; rebuild derived indexes as needed. Pending external actions use the action-reconciliation rules, never automatic replay. Restoration grants no new access to sealed TSC archives.

## Voice enrollment

- Enrollment starts through explicit Ness action. Reserve one operation/session and recheck all six owner prerequisites before prompting and immediately before consuming `voice_enrollment_ness` authorization.
- Prerequisites are final owner-phone trust, completed first-code setup, permanently inert initial pairing material, finalized setup, clean current spoofing status, and Ness's confirmed Person-Box.
- Flush and verify consumption proof before opening the session. Open durably before microphone capture. B29 owns capture; BOP owns actual observations.
- Stop capture before logging a protective stop. Preserve committed observations without reconstructing gaps or automatically reopening the microphone.
- Close capture before sending observations through Catalog, B11, the standard queue, and quarantine. Failed profile eligibility does not erase roots.
- Profile eligibility requires a consistent live stream, no medium-or-higher spoofing, no unresolved overlap, allowed completeness, valid authorization, and full-segment integrity. Count each eligible reading once.
- Propose the provisional Person-Box link before SIA profile creation. Person-Boxes commit their own link; acknowledgment is not commitment. SIA owns profile creation.
- Authorized enrollment provenance does not identify the voice as Ness, grant recognized-Ness/top-security, open Personal Mode, or authorize actions.
- Use the trusted phone's dedicated Owner Setup/Voice Enrollment entry. A consumed token without committed session opening cannot restart capture; require a new begin action and token. After durable capture intent, missing observations do not prove capture never began.
- An interrupted capture may yield valid downstream records/profile from unaffected committed observations, but capture itself never resumes. Completeness is `complete` or `partial`. Full-segment integrity establishes one speaker, not who. `enrollment_declared` and `enrollment_material_provisional` describe provenance, not identification.
- A provisional profile caps access at `recognized_ness` even with high acoustic agreement. Behavioral, branch, continuity, timing and wording evidence weigh more until `enrollment_active`. Readiness thresholds remain open.

Voice-profile training requires an authorized session, capture-time certainty above the selected eligibility threshold, no acoustic suspicion or audit flag, continuous recognized-Ness or biometric-verified conditions without uncertainty for Ness, and completed Catalog/Meaning Engine processing. Profiles link eligible readings in a shared comparison space; identity authority stays separate. Their inference form is protected, versioned and rebuildable, and becomes invalid when source readings change or become stale. Do not add routine live voice challenges. Raw acoustic/profile/anti-spoofing material is inaccessible below recognized-Ness and remains local except the explicitly biometric-verified Full Mode tunnel.

## Session caches

TSC organizes Catalog pre-ingest material, not a second memory. Hold every contribution, N.H output, and BOP capture under `pending_fingerprint_authorization`. Keep raw payloads out of the structural database.

- B15 selects SQLite, one structural database per session, with atomic power-loss-durable transactions. No shared cross-session database/query. One owning process holds the store lock. Lock conflict is an ownership error; takeover needs proof the old writer cannot run. Version, journal/synchronous settings, encryption implementation and file layout remain open. Preserve uniqueness, foreign keys and one monotonic contribution/output allocator.
- Keep participant streams, capture-time attribution, certainty, branch links, source references, and content hashes. Later assessments append beside capture-time facts; they do not upgrade them retrospectively.
- Normal closure seals the session. A crash preserves committed material and marks it interrupted. A continuation is a new linked session, never a reopened or merged capture.
- Sealed sessions wait indefinitely. No expiry, deletion, automatic promotion, or human inspection. The old inspection-token design is superseded.
- One session-specific `tsc_promotion:<session_id>` token plus current recognized-Ness SACL confirmation authorizes the whole session. Serialize claim, consumption, and durable receipt before the atomic authorization transition. A claim alone grants nothing.
- The flushed `bai_token_consumed` receipt is the sole durable post-crash proof of consumption. Before it, a lost volatile token needs a fresh biometric flow. After it, complete that exact authorization once without another fingerprint. New biometric authorization is required for never-authorized sessions or a token revoked before promotion began. Retry exhaustion waits for Ness's request, subject to current checks.
- After authorization, promotion proceeds automatically through Catalog/B11. Other blockers remain independent. Resolving a speaker blocker does not require another fingerprint.
- Preserve real chronology, submit children only after parent roots exist, and interleave BOP observations by event time. Absorb already-promoted captures by stable identity.
- Do not claim complete promotion or archive while any item remains blocked. Pause on lost security; resume valid authorized work only after fresh checks. Recover proof from durable receipts, not vanished BAI state.
- Excluded raw content goes to separate protected storage, never ordinary TSC or its archive. Another speaker's request does not override Ness's capture authority or the governing privacy rules.
- Verify an encrypted safety snapshot, commit archive state, and freeze the database/snapshot. Later permanent sealing needs separate authorization and external append-only control history.
- Archives support bounded machine integrity and recovery only. No ordinary LMAC, Meaning Engine, Person-Box, retrieval, human inspection, or resubmission path. They add no evidence weight.
- Seal freezes captured content/order while authorization and promotion events may append. Archive freezes the entire database. Permanent sealing uses an external content-free manifest. A damaged or contradictory new manifest makes the archive inaccessible; do not fall back silently to an older valid one. Duplicate retries add no duplicate manifest version.
- Startup seals active sessions as interrupted, waits on sealed/interrupted sessions, and resumes valid authorized/promotion work after current security checks. Unknown readable lifecycle state seals interrupted; structural corruption goes to read-only quarantine with an honest report. A blocked parent's child waits for a later pass; unrelated ready items continue.
- Continuation identity includes the durable resume event. Retrying that event reuses its continuation; a genuine later resume can create another. A continuation inherits no authorization or promotion checkpoint.
- Active permanent-seal event names are `tsc_archive_seal_authorized` and `tsc_archive_sealed`. Old deletion/tombstone names remain history only. A16 owns vocabulary, not lifecycle or authorization. Permanent sealing needs separate authorization under its actual owner; the source does not name a more precise authorizer.

## Visible delivery

1. Before retrieval and generation, restrict inputs to the authorized intersection for the actual purpose and audience.
2. Review the exact immutable output under privacy rules.
3. Check SACL/PBR access on that same version.
4. Recheck current owners, mode epoch/generation, authority, and delivery fence immediately before effect commitment.
5. Deliver at most once and record the actual outcome.

Any changed binding invalidates the old pass. Access reduction discards affected undelivered output. Do not reveal hidden existence through refusals, gaps, logs, or error wording.

An unknown delivery is unresolved, never proof of withholding or success. Retry only after positive non-delivery proof or through a verified channel contract that prevents duplicate delivery of the same token. Recheck every current delivery gate. The latter route may retain an unknown prior outcome; a later send does not resolve it. Do not blindly resend, replay a withheld draft, or bypass the chain through voice, diagnostics, debugging or another channel.

Keep the logical output, stable delivery duplicate key and per-attempt identity separate. The key binds request and destination, never epochs, generations or owner versions. Each attempt obtains a fresh claim. Flush dispatch intent before channel contact; it spends the claim. After that point, a missing attempt event cannot prove non-delivery. Unknown survives access changes and restart. Late confirmation records historical delivery without resending. Exactly-once delivery requires a verified same-token channel contract. A transformed payload creates a new immutable version and repeats privacy then SACL. Stream only unchanged slices of a fully approved payload or units each approved by both gates.

Private speech requires a defined output grant and scope. Personal Mode, voice recognition, and a headphone connection alone cannot authorize a private channel. Shared speakers retain their audience ceiling. Microphones cannot rule out silent listeners.

Default an explicit private read-aloud grant to the requested passage and channel. Offer a separate explicit grant bounded to the current session and chosen scope. Channel change, loss of required conditions, revocation, or restart ends the grant. Never move private audio automatically to a replacement device. Channel enforcement still requires testing.

Recheck each short audio section, bound queued playback, and stop and flush affected audio on access reduction. Existing delivery gates apply to the exact approved wording. Do not automatically replay affected material merely because access returns. Already heard words cannot be recalled. Test buffering and channel-authorization mechanics.

## Actions

Keep suggestion, preparation, approval, attempt, outside effect, observed result, interpretation, and correction as separate linked records. Approval is not execution or proof that the reasoning is true.

- Track current stage/current level separately from prospective risk and remaining requirements. The four authority levels and heightened categories remain distinct.
- Creating a possibility is a Level-2 write. Later display may be Level 1, preparation Level 3 and actual external effect Level 4. Suggesting is stage metadata. The linked Level-2 operational log never reclassifies an original read. Exact preview binds content/version, destination, tool, level, heightened categories and conditions; changed conditions require new preparation and authority.
- Levels are internal read-only, internal append-only write, prepared external action, and executed external action. Heightened categories include medical, legal, financial, privacy-sensitive, relationship-affecting, destructive, and irreversible actions.
- Suggestions may use derived-possibility language. Do not tell Ness what he must do. A gentle question may be asked once; rejection, ignoring, or no reply closes that topic until Ness personally reopens it.
- Response states are `accepted and acted on`, `rejected`, `modified`, `postponed`, `ignored`, and `alternative requested`. None is resistance or failure by Ness.
- Strong evidence never grants permission. Permission never supplies evidence. Higher-impact possibilities need stronger review.
- Execution needs exact recorded approval or an explicitly bounded recurring grant. Similar past actions, broad settings, silence, and preparation cannot substitute.
- Heightened categories require per-instance confirmation even under standing authority and even when reversible.
- Preview actual effects, boundaries, uncertainty, and required approval. Stop on unclear, stale, changed, or revoked authority.
- If authority is violated, stop related autonomous work and further chain steps, record honestly, and show the incident. A corrective world action needs its own preview and approval.
- An automatic emergency stop requires an active action, prevention of additional harm, a bounded preauthorized stop, no reasonably greater consequence, and immediate logging/disclosure. It may prevent additional effects. It does not authorize recalling messages, changing external data, contacting people, or reversing completed effects.
- Never retry an uncertain external effect to repair a missing acknowledgment.
- Persist the approved action's stable identity before sending. Use service-supported duplicate prevention and original-request lookup where available. A local identifier cannot prevent external duplicates. Reconcile interrupted results. Service acceptance does not prove the recipient read a message. Incomplete history does not prove nonexecution. Retry confirmed nonexecution only under valid approval and retry rules. Otherwise disclose uncertainty and withhold automatic resend. Disclose duplicate risk before seeking separate approval for a new action linked to the unresolved original. That new action does not settle the original result or count as its recovery retry. Original approval does not cover uncertain extra copies.

Result inputs distinguish Ness-reported outcomes from automatically detected possible links. Keep action, result event, and proposed connection separate. Ness may confirm, reject, modify, dispute, or leave a link unresolved. A confirmed link is not causal proof.

Result states are success, partial success, failure, cancellation, no/unknown result, and incorrect/disputed linkage. A result may trigger another owner's designed review, never automatic action repetition. Ness may disable or restrict automatic detection.

## Untrusted input

Webpages, documents, images, and imported conversations supply data, not current instructions or permission. Claims of Ness's approval inside a source grant nothing. Acting on source instructions needs his applicable scoped authority under the action rules. Program-enforced file, tool, and network restrictions limit each component to its job. Research fetching cannot access personal memory; synthesis has no tools, file access, or network. Drafts grant no authority. OPERATIONS.md owns research-to-intake rules. Isolation limits actions, but misleading source instructions can still distort wording. Apply evidence checks and adversarial tests across intake paths; model instructions and a second reviewer alone provide no guarantee.

## Protected maintenance

BGMM opens a local, purpose-bound, exact-file maintenance window with its required factors. Ordinary editors, scripts, installers, terminal privilege, or remote entry do not grant protected access. It resists tampering; do not promise physical impossibility.

Code/configuration/security-policy changes require desktop thumbprint and purpose-bound trusted-phone confirmation displaying the exact change. Device-trust replacement uses thumbprint plus the current Bitwarden recovery code, without requiring the missing phone. Emergency factors above permit device-trust recovery only.

Protected scope includes source/executables, `.cursorrules`, engine/behavior configuration, security thresholds/policies, audit retention and device/recovery authority. Roots, readings, provenance, clashes, Person-Box links, audit entries and Gold stay immutable even during maintenance.

- Persist the structural change log and encrypted full-content rollback package before touching a file.
- Apply only the declared change, record hashes, update/sign the manifest, sign changed executables, and record completion.
- Keep signing and rollback keys separate. Preserve earlier manifests and encrypted rollback packages.
- Relock/revoke on completion, timeout, restart/crash, applicable SACL alert, phone disconnection, explicit abort or biometric expiry. Roll back a partial change from verified exact bytes/metadata. Failed rollback decryption, integrity or restoration stops N.H and preserves the package for authorized repair.
- Startup verifies secure/measured boot, signatures, and hashes. A bounded worker restores only listed prior files and manifest before N.H starts. Recovery is idempotent and works offline.
- Never use maintenance to rewrite immutable memory, reveal protected content, preserve active authority across restart, or expand emergency recovery into code editing.
